02 / Watchdog job

Recovery, not chaos.

The watchdog checks once a minute, heals a dropped connection at most twice, and stops with a timestamped report when reality refuses to cooperate.

Bounded by design

A recovered outage resets its local restart budget. A later outage gets its own two attempts. A failed second recovery ends the loop.

Useful evidence

Every check, failure, restart, recovery, and give-up event is timestamped so diagnosis can start from facts.
