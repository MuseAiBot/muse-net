05 / Fair-use controls

Fair shares, measured pipes.

Each admitted guest gets the configured cap. Bursts are explicit. When the cap hits, traffic is shaped or delayed—not silently waved through.

Per guest

The rate limiter is instantiated for each admitted identity, so one noisy guest cannot quietly consume the whole policy.

Carrier reality

The local cap is not the phone plan. FedNet can shape local sharing, but it cannot expand the carrier’s underlying capacity.
