01 / Start job

Muse runs the room.

The upstream connection is a prerequisite, not the headline. The operator brings FedNet up, proves that the internet really works, and records evidence instead of vibes.

What it does

Starts the upstream pipe, checks the expected route, runs a real reachability probe, and reports a connected state only after the evidence is there.

What it does not do

It does not silently open FED-NET sharing. A ready pipe and an open gateway are different states on purpose.
